About the association

Vicbrew Incorporated

Vicbrew Incorporated is a registered Victorian not-for-profit incorporated association that connects homebrewers, clubs, competition organisers, judges, and stewards across Victoria.

Official website and domain

Vicbrew Incorporated owns and operates vicbrew.org as Vicbrew's official website, promoting amateur homebrewing across Victoria through competitions, club collaboration, and judging education.

Our purpose and activities

Vicbrew brings Victorian homebrew clubs together and organises the annual Victorian Amateur Brewing Championships. The association also supports open competitions, promotes consistent style and judging standards, and helps develop judges and stewards.

By bringing clubs and volunteers together, Vicbrew creates opportunities for brewers to share knowledge, develop their skills, and strengthen Victoria's homebrewing community.
